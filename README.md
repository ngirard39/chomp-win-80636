## Overview

CHOMP is a Pokemon VGC decision engine designed for browser use alongside Pokemon Showdown. It examines a team preview and recommends four Pokemon to bring, along with two lead selections, to support matchup planning before a battle begins.

Its Gen 9 damage model evaluates exact damage ranges and knockout probabilities across every damage roll. The analysis can account for weather, terrain, screens, held items, abilities, speed modifiers, Intimidate, immunities, and setup states. CHOMP also includes replay analysis for reviewing Showdown games and comparing selected teams.

## Script Features

- Suggests four Pokemon to bring from the available team preview.
- Recommends two leading Pokemon for the opening position.
- Calculates Gen 9 damage across all possible damage rolls.
- Reports knockout probability alongside calculated damage results.
- Models weather, terrain, screens, items, abilities, and speed changes.
- Includes battle effects such as Intimidate, immunities, and setup conditions.
- Analyzes Pokemon Showdown replays and compares team selections.
- Provides a standalone calculator covering 289 formes and 299 moves.
- Supports both classic and beta Pokemon Showdown clients.
